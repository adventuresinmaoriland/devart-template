Tāpiritia

Extend your te reo Māori beyond simple sentences

You can already say:

Ka haere au ki te mahi.

now try

Ka haere au ki te mahi, engari kāore au e hiahia ana.

I'm going to work, but I don't want to.

Small words like engari, hei, and ā muri i tērā unlock richer, more natural kōrero. This app helps you practise using them.
